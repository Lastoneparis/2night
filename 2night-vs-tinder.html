2NIGHT vs Tinder: two different ideas of when a date happens

By the 2NIGHT team · Updated 4 September 2026

2NIGHT is a one-night dating app for iPhone that shows who is out near you tonight, so you can match and meet the same evening instead of chatting for days.

2NIGHT is an alternative to Tinder, Bumble, Happn and Pure for people who want a spontaneous same-night date. Tinder is the largest dating app in the world and it is not going anywhere. The question this page answers is narrower: on an evening when you want to meet someone tonight, which of the two is built for it, and what do you trade away?

The core difference

Tinder's model is a standing catalogue. Profiles are permanent, the distance filter reaches up to roughly 160 km (100 miles), and whether the person is free this evening is something you find out by asking. 2NIGHT's model is a night: 2NIGHT uses a radius around your location and a nightly “Tonight” status that expires at 5 AM; your profile only appears in the feed while you are live, next to other people who are out that night.

Where Tinder is better

  • Reach. Tinder is live in essentially every city on earth. On 2NIGHT the header count is honest and can be small while a city is opening.
  • Verification and moderation scale. Optional photo and ID verification, and a large safety team. 2NIGHT's safety queue is read by a person, but there is no verification at all.
  • Profile depth. If you want to filter on lifestyle, values and prompts before meeting, Tinder gives you far more than 150 characters and six photos.
  • Platforms. Android and web.

Where 2NIGHT is better

  • Timing is solved by design. Everyone in the feed went live tonight. No “are you free this week?”.
  • Distance you can act on. A 5 km radius is a plan; a 160 km one is a wish list.
  • Chats with a deadline. 48 hours to name a place. Then it closes.
  • Plans. A public invitation nearby people can join — there is no equivalent on Tinder.
  • Privacy posture. No ads, no analytics SDK, rounded distances only, location blurred after 30 days idle, chats deleted with the account.

Price, honestly

Both apps are free to use with limits. Tinder's paid tiers add unlimited likes, Passport, rewinds, seeing who liked you and priority likes; its prices vary by age and country and are best checked in the app. 2NIGHT's free Silver tier includes a nightly allowance of pings, a 5 km radius and one active plan; Gold adds unlimited pings, 15 km, a weekly Boost, Super Pings, Travel Mode, private albums and chat relaunch; Platinum Black adds 50 km, priority placement and Black Events. Every 2NIGHT price is shown in the app before you pay, and we do not publish numbers here so that this page cannot go stale or contradict your storefront.

Verdict

Use Tinder for the wide, slow search. Use 2NIGHT on the evening you want to be out with someone real before the night is over. Many people will do both, and that is fine. Related reading: a Tinder alternative for tonight and our 2026 comparison of tonight-focused apps.

Frequently asked questions

Is 2NIGHT just Tinder for hookups?

No. 2NIGHT is a same-night meeting app: you say what you are up for tonight (drinks, food, music, a walk…) and whether you are open to dating, friends or both. It is about being out tonight, not about a specific kind of encounter.

Does 2NIGHT have Passport like Tinder?

Travel Mode on Gold and Platinum Black lets you set your status in any city before you arrive; the feed re-centres on that city.

Which app is safer?

Tinder offers optional photo and ID verification and has a large safety team; 2NIGHT has neither verification nor automated chat scanning, but has human-reviewed reports, silent mutual blocking, a two-minute video call and a strong privacy posture. On both, the real safety system is meeting in public and telling a friend.

Can I use 2NIGHT on Android or on the web?

No. 2NIGHT is iPhone only. Tinder runs on iOS, Android and the web.
