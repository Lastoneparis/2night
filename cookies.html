Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how 2night.co uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help a website function.

Cookies we use

2night.co uses only strictly necessary cookies — those required for the site to work securely (e.g. session, security, load-balancing). These are exempt from prior consent under the ePrivacy Directive because the site cannot function without them.

We do not use advertising, marketing, profiling, or third-party analytics cookies. We do not track you across sites.

Third-party resources

Some pages may load assets (fonts, scripts) from third-party CDNs to render correctly. These do not set marketing cookies. We are progressively self-hosting these to minimise data transfer.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the site.
