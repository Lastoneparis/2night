Your privacy

Your privacy choices

You're in control of your data on 2NIGHT. Here's how to exercise your privacy choices, anytime.

01

Access your data

Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and we'll send it to you.

02

Delete your account & data

Delete your account anytime in Settings → Account → Delete account, or email us. We erase your data, subject to any legal retention.

03

Opt out of marketing

Turn off promotional emails and push notifications in Settings → Notifications, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

04

Do not sell or share

We never sell your personal data and never share it for cross-context behavioural advertising — there's nothing to opt out of.

05

Manage cookies

Change your cookie and tracking preferences anytime through the cookie banner or your browser settings.

06

Location & permissions

Control location, camera and notification access from your device settings, and revoke any of them whenever you want.

These choices are available to everyone, everywhere — including the rights granted by the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California). We'll never charge you or treat you differently for using them.

To exercise a privacy right, email privacy@2night.co — we respond within 30 days.
