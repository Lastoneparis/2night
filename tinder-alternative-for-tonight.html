A Tinder alternative for tonight: what changes when the app is built for one evening

By the 2NIGHT team · Updated 4 September 2026

2NIGHT is a one-night dating app for iPhone that shows who is out near you tonight, so you can match and meet the same evening instead of chatting for days.

Tinder is very good at what it was built for: a huge pool, a fast swipe, and a match that may become a date at some point. If your goal is a date tonight, though, its shape works against you — the pool is wide, the timing is unknown, and the chat is where plans go to fade. This page is for people searching for a Tinder alternative that starts from the evening, not the profile. 2NIGHT is an alternative to Tinder, Bumble, Happn and Pure for people who want a spontaneous same-night date.

The structural difference

Tinder's unit is the profile: it exists whether or not you are free, and the distance filter reaches up to roughly 160 km. Its paid tiers — Plus, Gold and Platinum — add unlimited likes, Passport, seeing who liked you, Top Picks and priority likes; list prices in the United States run from about $24.99 to $49.99 a month and vary by age and location. Tinder offers optional Photo Verification with a video selfie and, in some markets, ID verification.

2NIGHT's unit is the night. 2NIGHT uses a radius around your location and a nightly “Tonight” status that expires at 5 AM; your profile only appears in the feed while you are live, next to other people who are out that night. Where Tinder answers “who might I like?”, 2NIGHT answers “who is out, near me, right now — and what are they up for?”.

What you gain by switching for a night

  • Shared availability by default. Everyone in the feed went live tonight. The hardest part of any plan is solved before the first message.
  • A radius that means something. Five kilometres is a walk or a short ride. You are never shown someone you could not realistically meet before closing time.
  • A conversation with a deadline. Forty-eight hours is enough to pick a bar; it is not enough to become pen-pals.
  • Plans. When a one-to-one date feels like a lot, post where you will be and let people join.
  • A video check. Two minutes of video, device to device, before you leave the house.

What you give up — honestly

  • Scale. Tinder is everywhere. 2NIGHT is opening city by city and, on a quiet night in a small city, the honest header count may be low.
  • Verification. Tinder's photo and ID checks are optional but real. 2NIGHT has none; your judgement is the safety system, and we say so on every page.
  • Android and web. 2NIGHT is iPhone only.
  • Deep profiles. If you want to filter on values and lifestyle before meeting, Tinder's and Bumble's profiles do more. 2NIGHT gives you 150 characters, six photos, tonight's categories and a status line.

Safety and moderation on 2NIGHT

Report from a profile (the shield icon) or from the ⋯ menu in a chat, with one of three reasons; a person reads every report, usually within 24 hours and always within 72, and may remove the account, warn it or close the report. Reports are confidential by design — no member, including the reporter, can read them back. Blocking is instant, silent and mutual, reversible under Settings → Safety & privacy. Nobody reads your chats, and messages are deleted with your account. There is no background check; meet in public and tell a friend.

Using both

It is not either/or. Plenty of people will keep a Tinder profile for the slow search and open 2NIGHT on the evenings they want company now. Frequently asked questions

Is 2NIGHT a replacement for Tinder?

For a same-night date, yes — that is what it is built for. For a slow, profile-led search for a long-term partner, Tinder or Bumble remain better tools. Many people will use both.

Does 2NIGHT have something like Tinder Passport?

Yes: Travel Mode, on the Gold and Platinum Black tiers, lets you set your status in any city before you arrive.

Does 2NIGHT verify photos like Tinder?

No. There is no photo or ID verification and no badge. The two-minute video call is the practical substitute.

How much does 2NIGHT cost compared with Tinder?

Joining and meeting people on 2NIGHT is free. Gold and Platinum Black memberships are billed by Apple and their prices are shown in the app before you pay. Tinder's US list prices run from about $24.99 to $49.99 a month and vary by age and location.

Is 2NIGHT on Android?

No, iPhone only. Tinder is on iOS, Android and the web.
