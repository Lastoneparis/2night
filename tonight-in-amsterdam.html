Tonight in Amsterdam: a one-night dating app for a city you cross by bike

By the 2NIGHT team · Updated 7 September 2026

2NIGHT is a one-night dating app for iPhone that shows who is out near you tonight, so you can match and meet the same evening instead of chatting for days.

Amsterdam is small, flat and crossed by bicycle, which changes what “near me” means. A match 4 km away in most cities is a metro ride; in Amsterdam it is fifteen minutes of pedalling along a canal. 2NIGHT shows you the people who are live tonight within a distance you set, and in this city that distance can be the whole town.

How a night out works in Amsterdam

The Amsterdam evening starts early and stays civilised on weeknights. The borrel, an after-work drink, begins around 5 or 6; dinner is at 7 or 8; and the brown cafés and bars are at their fullest between 9 and midnight. Cafés and bars in the centre are generally allowed to stay open until 1 am on weeknights and until 3 or 4 am at weekends, with clubs later, so a Wednesday has a natural end around 1 and a Saturday does not.

Where people go depends on whether they live here. Visitors gather around Leidseplein and Rembrandtplein, the two nightlife squares, and in the old centre around De Wallen. Amsterdammers go out closer to home: in the Jordaan, in De Pijp south of the canal ring, in Oud-West around the Kinkerstraat, in Oost around the Javastraat, in Westerpark, and increasingly across the water in Noord, where the ferry from Central Station lands you in a different city. If you are staying in the centre, going live from there works on any night, but a status line that says “De Pijp, drinks from 9” tells locals you know the difference.

The city inside the A10 ring road is roughly 10 km across, and the canal ring at its heart is barely 2 km wide. It is possible to be out on a Friday and, by midnight, have cycled through four neighbourhoods.

Radius settings that make sense in Amsterdam

2NIGHT uses a radius around your location and a nightly “Tonight” status that expires at 5 AM; your profile only appears in the feed while you are live, next to other people who are out that night. The max-distance slider starts at 1 km and is capped at 5 km on the free Silver tier, 15 km on Gold and 50 km on Platinum Black. In Amsterdam:

  • 1–2 km: the canal ring and one adjoining neighbourhood. From the Jordaan that reaches the centre and Westerpark; from De Pijp it covers the Museum quarter and the southern canals, all on foot.
  • 3 km: the centre, Jordaan, De Pijp, Oud-West and Oost in one circle. This is the everyday setting: every match is a ten-minute bike ride.
  • 5 km (Silver cap): essentially everything inside the A10 ring plus the near side of Noord. The free tier covers the whole Amsterdam most people go out in.
  • 15 km and 50 km (Gold, Platinum Black): Amstelveen, Haarlem, Zaandam and the rest of the region, then most of the Randstad. For people living outside the city and for quiet nights.

Because the city is small and cycling makes it smaller, the honest advice is to keep the circle at 3 to 5 km and to name the neighbourhood in your status line.

Bikes, night buses and the ferry across the IJ

The transport reality in Amsterdam is that most people cycle home, whatever the hour, and the rest take the night bus. Trams and the metro wind down around 12:30 to 1 am, after which the night buses (the N lines) run from Central Station and Leidseplein through the night, roughly every half hour. Taxis and app cars are available but slow to find at 3 am on a Saturday. The free ferries across the IJ to Noord run around the clock, which is why Noord's nightlife works at all. Practically: on a weeknight the 1 am closing time sets the end of the evening; if you cycled, remember that the same rules about drink apply on a bicycle as in a car, and if you did not, agree on a place near a night-bus stop that serves you both.

Weeknight versus weekend on 2NIGHT

The “Tonight” status expires at 5 AM, and the chat window lasts 48 hours. On a weeknight, go live at borrel time with a category (drinks, food, walk, talk, coffee) and a status that names the neighbourhood; pings sent between 6 and 10 are the ones that become a table before the bars close at 1. At the weekend, later is fine, the feed leans toward nightlife and music after midnight, and a Plan (“terrace in Westerpark from 8, come say hoi”) is often the easiest way to bring two or three people together. If a Thursday match did not happen, the 48-hour chat is still open on Friday.

Safety on a same-night date in Amsterdam

2NIGHT does not verify identities or photos and runs no background checks, so the habits on our safety page do the work: meet in a busy, well-lit public place, never a private home or a car; tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting; keep your own way home; never send money, gift cards or crypto to anyone you met in the app. In Amsterdam that means a café on a busy street rather than a houseboat, a bike route you know, and a night bus in mind if you are not cycling. Any match can start a two-minute peer-to-peer video call before you leave, free during the launch period. Block is instant, silent and mutual; reports (inappropriate content, fake or scam, harassment) are read by a person within 24 to 72 hours. Other members only ever see a rounded distance in kilometres, never your address.

What radius should I set in Amsterdam?

3 km from the centre, the Jordaan or De Pijp covers most of the city's nightlife by bike. The free tier's 5 km cap already reaches nearly everything inside the A10 ring; Gold (15 km) and Platinum Black (50 km) cover the region.

How do people get home late in Amsterdam?

Mostly by bike. Trams and the metro wind down around 12:30 to 1 am; night buses run through the night from Central Station and Leidseplein, and the free ferries to Noord run around the clock.

The Jordaan, De Pijp, Oud-West and Oost for locals; Leidseplein, Rembrandtplein and the old centre for visitors. Noord and Westerpark have their own scenes, busier at weekends.
