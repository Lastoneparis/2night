Support

How can we help?

Everything you need to know about 2NIGHT — and a real human at the other end when you need one.

Is 2NIGHT free?

Yes. Joining 2NIGHT and meeting people is free. A paid membership is on the way, and you'll see exactly what it costs in the app before anything is charged.

Who can see that I'm out?

Only people inside the radius and audience you choose, and only while you're live. Go offline and you disappear.

How does matching work?

Ping someone you'd like to meet. If they ping back, it's a match and a chat opens — so you can make a plan for tonight.

How do memberships work?

Any paid membership is billed through the App Store and renews automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store settings.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can export or delete your account and data anytime from Settings, or email us and we'll take care of it.
