Tonight in Berlin: a one-night dating app for a city with no closing time

By the 2NIGHT team · Updated 7 September 2026

2NIGHT is a one-night dating app for iPhone that shows who is out near you tonight, so you can match and meet the same evening instead of chatting for days.

Berlin is the city where “tonight” has the loosest definition in Europe. There is no legal closing time for bars, the trains run all night at weekends, and a Friday can start at midnight and end on Sunday. That freedom is wonderful and it makes meeting someone new oddly hard, because nobody is in a hurry. 2NIGHT gives the night a shape: the people who are live within your radius, this evening, until 5 AM.

How a night out works in Berlin

On a weeknight Berlin is a bar city, and a neighbourhood one. People drink where they live: in the Kneipen and cocktail bars of Kreuzberg and Neukölln, along the canal between the two, in Friedrichshain around Boxhagener Platz, in Prenzlauer Berg for a quieter evening, in Schöneberg around Nollendorfplatz, the historic heart of the city's queer nightlife, and increasingly in Wedding. Mitte has bars too, but it is where visitors stay rather than where Berliners go out. A Tuesday runs from about 8 pm to 1 or 2; a beer from a Späti on a bench by the canal is as legitimate a plan as any bar.

Weekends are a different animal. Clubs open late and run through the morning, the crowd moves toward the Spree around Friedrichshain and Kreuzberg, and it is normal to leave home at midnight. If you go live at 8 pm on a Saturday, expect the feed to keep changing until 3; if you go live at 1 am, you are on time. The categories in the app (drinks, music, nightlife, talk, walk) matter more in Berlin than anywhere, because “out tonight” can mean a quiet glass of wine or a very long night, and it is kinder to say which.

The city is also big: about 45 km east to west, nearly 900 square kilometres, with the Ringbahn S-Bahn circle marking the inner city. Kreuzberg to Prenzlauer Berg is a real journey, and someone in Charlottenburg is, for the purposes of a spontaneous drink, in another town.

Radius settings that make sense in Berlin

2NIGHT uses a radius around your location and a nightly “Tonight” status that expires at 5 AM; your profile only appears in the feed while you are live, next to other people who are out that night. The max-distance slider starts at 1 km and is capped at 5 km on the free Silver tier, 15 km on Gold and 50 km on Platinum Black. In Berlin:

  • 1–3 km: one Kiez and its neighbours. From Kottbusser Tor that is most of Kreuzberg and north Neukölln, walkable or one U-Bahn stop; from Boxhagener Platz it is Friedrichshain and the river.
  • 5 km (Silver cap): Kreuzberg, Neukölln, Friedrichshain and Mitte together, or Prenzlauer Berg with Mitte and Wedding. This is the weeknight setting: every match is one direct train away.
  • 15 km (Gold): everything inside the Ringbahn and a good way beyond, including Charlottenburg and Schöneberg from the east. Useful on a slow Monday or if you live outside the ring.
  • 50 km (Platinum Black): the whole of Berlin and Potsdam. It is there for people outside the city and for very quiet nights.

Name the Kiez in your status line. “Neukölln, free from 9” does more work in Berlin than in any other city on this list, because the neighbourhood tells people what kind of night you mean.

Trains all night at the weekend, night buses in the week

Berlin's transport is built for its nightlife. On Friday and Saturday nights the U-Bahn and S-Bahn run through the night, roughly every fifteen minutes, so a weekend match across the city is genuinely reachable at 3 am. On Sunday to Thursday nights most U-Bahn lines stop around 1 am and are replaced by night buses that follow the same routes, while the Metro tram and bus lines keep running around the clock. Taxis and app cars are easy to find. Practically: on a weeknight, meet in the Kiez where one of you already is and let the last U-Bahn be the end; at the weekend the trains remove the deadline, and the only limit is the 5 AM reset of your “Tonight” status.

Weeknight versus weekend on 2NIGHT

The “Tonight” status expires at 5 AM and the chat window lasts 48 hours, which suits Berlin's two speeds. On a weeknight, go live around 7 or 8 with a category and a status that names the Kiez, and ping early; the useful window closes around midnight. At the weekend, later is fine, and a Plan (“canal in Kreuzberg from 10, bring a drink”) is often the most Berlin way to meet: low key, open, no pressure. If your Friday match did not happen because the night went elsewhere, the 48-hour chat is still open on Saturday.

Safety on a same-night date in Berlin

2NIGHT does not verify identities or photos and runs no background checks, so the habits on our safety page do the work: meet in a busy, well-lit public place, never a private home or a car; tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting; keep your own way home; never send money, gift cards or crypto to anyone you met in the app. Berlin's nights are long and loose, so decide in advance when you are going home and stick to it; a busy bar or a bench by the canal with people around is a first meet, a private flat is not. Any match can start a two-minute peer-to-peer video call before you leave, free during the launch period. Block is instant, silent and mutual; reports (inappropriate content, fake or scam, harassment) are read by a person within 24 to 72 hours. Other members only ever see a rounded distance in kilometres, never your address.
