Tonight in Barcelona: a one-night dating app for a city that eats at ten

By the 2NIGHT team · Updated 7 September 2026

2NIGHT is a one-night dating app for iPhone that shows who is out near you tonight, so you can match and meet the same evening instead of chatting for days.

Barcelona runs two hours behind the rest of Europe. Dinner is at ten, the bars are quiet before eleven, and a night that starts at midnight is not late. It is also a compact, walkable city laid out on a grid, with the sea at the bottom and the hills at the top. Both facts matter for 2NIGHT: the radius can stay small, and the evening is long enough that a ping at nine can still be a drink at eleven.

How a night out works in Barcelona

The evening begins with a vermut or a beer on a terrace around 7 or 8, then dinner, then bars from about 11 to 2 or 3, then clubs for those who want them until dawn. On a weeknight the middle part is what matters: people meet for dinner and a couple of drinks in Gràcia, around the squares that give the neighbourhood its village feel; in El Born and the Gòtic in the old city; in Poble-sec at the foot of Montjuïc; in Sant Antoni and the Eixample, including the “Gaixample” blocks that are the centre of the city's LGBTQ nightlife. El Raval is louder and later. Poblenou, by the beach, has its own quieter scene and a younger crowd.

Weekends bring visitors into the old city and locals out into their own barris, and the timetable shifts later still. A Saturday in Barcelona often means dinner at 10:30, a bar at 12:30 and a decision about a club at 2:30, which is when most bars are required to close. If you are staying near the Rambla or the beach, go live from there by all means, but a status line that says “Gràcia, drinks from 11” will pull replies from people who live the city rather than pass through it.

Geographically the city is small, about 100 square kilometres and roughly 10 km across, hemmed in by the sea, the river and the Collserola hills. Everything from the Barceloneta to Gràcia is inside a 4 km circle drawn from Plaça de Catalunya.

Radius settings that make sense in Barcelona

2NIGHT uses a radius around your location and a nightly “Tonight” status that expires at 5 AM; your profile only appears in the feed while you are live, next to other people who are out that night. The max-distance slider starts at 1 km and is capped at 5 km on the free Silver tier, 15 km on Gold and 50 km on Platinum Black. In Barcelona:

  • 1–2 km: one barri and its neighbours. From Plaça del Sol in Gràcia that reaches the upper Eixample; from the Born it covers the Gòtic, the Raval and the Barceloneta on foot.
  • 3 km: the old city, the Eixample, Gràcia and Poble-sec in one circle. This is the everyday setting; every match is walkable or two metro stops away.
  • 5 km (Silver cap): nearly the whole city, from the beach to the edge of the hills, Sants to Poblenou. The free tier already covers the Barcelona most people go out in.
  • 15 km and 50 km (Gold, Platinum Black): L'Hospitalet, Badalona, Sant Cugat and the rest of the metropolitan area, then the coast in both directions. For people living outside the city and for very quiet nights.

Keep the circle tight and let the status line do the work. Because dinner is late, “free from 11” is a normal thing to write here, and it helps people plan around their own table.

Metro until midnight, until two, or all night

Barcelona's metro has three timetables and it is worth knowing them. Sunday to Thursday the last trains run around midnight. On Fridays and the eves of public holidays it runs until about 2 am. On Saturday night it runs all night. After the metro stops, the NitBus night buses run across the city from Plaça de Catalunya and along the main avenues, taxis are plentiful and the Bicing bikes are there if you are steady enough. Practically: on a weeknight, the midnight cut-off is early for a city that eats at ten, so agree a place you can both walk or bus home from and let the night run past the last train on foot; on Saturday the timetable is not a constraint at all.

Weeknight versus weekend on 2NIGHT

The “Tonight” status expires at 5 AM, which in Barcelona is when the last clubs are emptying, and the chat window lasts 48 hours. On a weeknight, go live around 8 with a category (drinks, food, walk, talk, rooftop) and a status that names the barri; pings between 8 and 11 are the ones that turn into a table. At the weekend, going live late is fine, and a Plan (“terrace in Poble-sec from 11, come by”) fits a city that likes to go out in groups. If a Friday ping did not become a meeting, the 48-hour chat is still open on Saturday.

Safety on a same-night date in Barcelona

2NIGHT does not verify identities or photos and runs no background checks, so the habits on our safety page do the work: meet in a busy, well-lit public place, never a private home or a car; tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting; keep your own way home; never send money, gift cards or crypto to anyone you met in the app. Barcelona's crowded streets are a good place to meet and a bad place to be careless with a phone or a bag; keep both close in the old city late at night. Any match can start a two-minute peer-to-peer video call before you leave, free during the launch period. Block is instant, silent and mutual; reports (inappropriate content, fake or scam, harassment) are read by a person within 24 to 72 hours. Other members only ever see a rounded distance in kilometres, never your address.
