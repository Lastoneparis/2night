Safety

Meet people the smart way.

2NIGHT is built for spontaneous, real-life nights. A few simple habits keep them fun — and keep you in control when you meet someone new.

01

Meet in a public place

For a first meet, pick somewhere busy and well-lit. Stay in public until you genuinely feel comfortable.

02

Tell a friend your plans

Let someone you trust know where you're going, who you're meeting, and when you'll be back.

03

Never send money

Never send money, gift cards, or crypto to someone you met on the app, no matter the story.

04

Trust your gut

If something feels off, it probably is. End the chat, leave the meet — there's no wrong reason to walk away.

05

Report & block

Anyone who makes you uncomfortable can be blocked and reported in a tap. Flagging keeps the night safer for everyone.

06

You're in control

You decide when you're live, how far your radius reaches, and what you share. We don't run background checks, so your own judgement comes first.

In an emergency, call for help.

If you ever feel unsafe, contact your local emergency services first. To report someone to us, email our team — we read every message.
