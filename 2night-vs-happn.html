2NIGHT vs Happn: crossed paths in the past, or out together tonight?

By the 2NIGHT team · Updated 4 September 2026

2NIGHT is a one-night dating app for iPhone that shows who is out near you tonight, so you can match and meet the same evening instead of chatting for days.

2NIGHT is an alternative to Tinder, Bumble, Happn and Pure for people who want a spontaneous same-night date. Happn is the closest of the big apps to 2NIGHT in spirit, because both are built on proximity. The difference is when. Happn shows you people you have physically crossed paths with — a timeline of the past. 2NIGHT shows you people who are live near you right now — a feed of tonight.

How each one uses location

Happn keeps location running in the background and logs an encounter whenever another Happn user comes within roughly 250 metres of you. Those people appear on your timeline, with a count of how many times you crossed and roughly where. Like someone; if they like you back it is a “Crush” and you can chat. There is no time limit on the timeline or the chat.

2NIGHT reads your location only while you are live, at roughly kilometre accuracy. 2NIGHT uses a radius around your location and a nightly “Tonight” status that expires at 5 AM; your profile only appears in the feed while you are live, next to other people who are out that night. The distance is set by you on a slider from 1 km up to your tier's cap (5 km free, 15 km Gold, 50 km Platinum Black), and other members see only a rounded distance in kilometres. Gold and Platinum Black can also re-centre the feed on any city with Travel Mode.

What Happn does better

  • Serendipity. Seeing that you passed the same person three times outside the same bakery is a genuinely good conversation starter, and no other app has it.
  • Verification. Happn's video certification gives you a badge; 2NIGHT has no verification at all.
  • Scale and Android. Happn is established across Europe and beyond, on both platforms.

What 2NIGHT does better

  • Present tense. Happn tells you who was near you; 2NIGHT tells you who is out near you tonight and what they are up for.
  • No background tracking. Happn's model needs your location running in the background. 2NIGHT uses it only while you are live, and you can hide yourself with one tap for free.
  • A deadline. Statuses reset at 5 AM and chats close after 48 hours, so conversations turn into plans or end.
  • Plans and video. A public invitation nearby people can join, and a two-minute video call to check someone is real.

Which one for which night

If you enjoy the idea of reconnecting with the person you keep seeing on your commute, and you are happy for that to take a few days, Happn is the right tool. If it is 7 pm, you are free and you would like to meet someone before midnight, 2NIGHT is built for exactly that. Frequently asked questions

Is 2NIGHT like Happn?

Both use proximity, but Happn shows people you crossed paths with in the past while 2NIGHT shows people who are live near you tonight, within a radius you set.

Does 2NIGHT track my location in the background?

No. Location is used only while you are live, at roughly kilometre accuracy, and other members see only a rounded distance. After about 30 days without going live your stored position is blurred to your city.

Does 2NIGHT have a verification badge like Happn?

No. 2NIGHT does not verify identity or photos. The two-minute video call is the practical alternative.

Which is cheaper?

Both are free to use with limits. Happn Premium is reported around $24.99–$29.99 a month depending on region; 2NIGHT's Gold and Platinum Black prices are shown in the app before you pay.
