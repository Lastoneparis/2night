Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

2NIGHT ("we", "us") is a location-based social-discovery app. This policy explains what we collect, why, the legal bases we rely on, and the choices and rights you have. We designed 2NIGHT to collect only what is necessary to help you meet people for real plans tonight.

Who we are

2NIGHT is operated by an independent operator based in France, who is the controller of your personal data. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at support@2night.co. We will provide our full legal name and registered address on request and where required by law.

Information we collect

Account details you provide (name, age, email), your status and plans, approximate and precise location while you are live, profile photos, and messages you send through the app. We also process basic device and usage data (such as device type, app version and diagnostic logs) to keep the service secure and reliable.

How we use your information

To show you who's out nearby, deliver the Tonight Feed, enable pings and matches, operate plans and chat, process membership subscriptions, keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with our legal obligations, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process your data on these bases: performance of our contract with you (to provide accounts, the Tonight Feed, matching, plans and chat); your consent (for precise geolocation, which you can withdraw at any time via your device settings or by going offline; and for any optional analytics, if introduced); our legitimate interests (to keep 2NIGHT secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the product, balanced against your rights); and compliance with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

Location

Location is central to 2NIGHT. You control when you are live. When you set your status, we use your location — with your consent — to surface relevant people near you within the radius your tier allows. You can stop sharing location at any time from your device settings or by going offline in the app.

Sharing and service providers

We share data with service providers who process it on our behalf under data-processing agreements, such as our cloud hosting and database/authentication provider (Supabase) and Apple (subscription billing and app distribution). Other members see only the profile, status, photos and information you choose to make visible. We may disclose data where required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our members or the public.

International data transfers

We process data primarily within the European Union. Because 2NIGHT operates worldwide, your data may also be processed in other countries. Where we transfer personal data outside the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum / Swiss equivalents) or an adequacy decision. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards by contacting us.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. In general: account and profile data are kept while your account is active; precise live-location data is kept only transiently and deleted or de-identified within about 30 days; messages are retained while your account is active and for up to 12 months after deletion for safety, dispute and legal purposes; and certain records may be kept longer where required by law or to defend legal claims. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within about 30 days, except data we must retain by law.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, export (portability), object to, and restrict our processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can exercise these rights in the app (Settings → export or delete) or by emailing support@2night.co; we will respond within the time required by law (generally one month under the GDPR). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority — in France, the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés, www.cnil.fr).

California (CCPA / CPRA) notice

In the past 12 months we have collected the categories of personal information described in "Information we collect" (identifiers, account details, precise geolocation, photos, communications and device/usage data) for the purposes described above. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. California residents have the right to know, access, correct and delete their personal information, to opt out of any sale or sharing, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information, without discrimination. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@2night.co. Because we do not sell or share personal information, no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" action is required; if this ever changes, we will provide that option here.

Adults only (18+)

2NIGHT is strictly for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use 2NIGHT. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it and terminate the account. If you believe a minor is using 2NIGHT, contact us at support@2night.co.

How we protect your data

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect your personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and restricting access on a need-to-know basis. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website currently uses only essential, first-party storage — needed for the site to work and to remember your choices, such as your language and your cookie preference (stored in your browser as tn_cookie_consent). We do not currently load analytics or advertising cookies. If we introduce optional analytics in the future, we will not set those cookies until you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser storage. Declining optional cookies still lets you use the full site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@2night.co.
